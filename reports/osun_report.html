What this page does and does not contain

IReV publishes photographs of the polling-unit result sheet, not machine-readable tallies. The portal's data carries no vote figures, so nothing here totals votes by party or candidate. Every count on this page is a count of result sheets uploaded. To read votes, open a sheet: the EC8A records registered voters, accredited voters, each party's score, and the presiding officer's certification.

Coverage is a snapshot, not a final figure. Uploads were still arriving when this was captured, and a unit marked awaiting may simply not have been photographed yet.
